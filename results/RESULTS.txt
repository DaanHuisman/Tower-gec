1. WHAT WAS RUN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question: if GEC data is added to TowerInstruct-7B's training mix, does GEC improve, and at what cost
to translation?

All three runs continue training the released TowerInstruct-7B-v0.1 for 2 epochs on the SAME fixed
30,000-example TowerBlocks replay subset (fingerprint md5=beea7a1bfd76). They differ only in the GEC data
added on top. Learning rate 2e-6 (cosine, 20 warmup steps), batch 256, full fine-tune on 4x H100.

  official  TowerInstruct-7B-v0.1 as released, no extra training (the baseline)
  control   + replay only                                  -> effect of extra training by itself
  it        + replay + Italian GEC (MultiGEC MERLIN, 651 essays)
  zh        + replay + Chinese GEC (MuCGEC Lang-8/HSK, 2,610 sentence pairs, token-matched to Italian)
  (it+zh, both languages together, was not run.)

Test sets: GEC - Italian MultiGEC-2025 dev (81 essays, held out of training), Chinese MuCGEC dev
(1,137 sentences, multiple references). MT - FLORES-200 devtest, WMT23, TICO-19 (37,669 segments).
Metrics: official scorers - MultiGEC-2025 GLEU + ERRANT P/R/F0.5 (Italian), ChERRANT char-level
P/R/F0.5 (Chinese); COMET-22 (wmt22-comet-da) + chrF for MT. Greedy decoding.

4. MAIN FINDINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Italian GEC improves strongly with Italian GEC data: F0.5 45.79 -> 56.36 (+10.6 vs official, +12.6 vs control),
  GLEU 58.82 -> 72.76 (+13.9 vs official).
  Mostly recall: 24.4 -> 47.9 (vs control), with precision also up.
* Chinese GEC improves with Chinese GEC data: F0.5 23.20 -> 27.90 (+4.7 vs official, +4.5 vs control),
  but by becoming more conservative:
  precision 26.0 -> 42.5, recall 16.8 -> 11.8, unchanged outputs 30% -> 40% (vs control).
* Cross-lingual: Italian GEC data also raises Chinese F0.5 by +2.6 vs control (the model edits more:
  19% of Chinese inputs unchanged vs 30%). Chinese GEC data changes Italian F0.5 by +0.9
  vs control - not significant (paired bootstrap p = .48, see SIGNIFICANCE.md).
* Extra training alone (control) changes Italian F0.5 by -2.0 vs official, so control is
  the fairer comparison point for the GEC runs.
* No measurable translation cost from GEC data: in the aggregated scores (section 3, first table) the GEC
  runs are within 0.12 COMET of control on every test set and direction. The small drop vs official
  (<= 0.22 aggregated; up to 0.42 for WMT23 en-zh) appears in control too, i.e. it comes from the extra
  training, not from the GEC data.

5. CAVEATS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* One training run per condition. GPU training is not bit-for-bit deterministic: two identical smoke runs
  differed by 0.40 F0.5 (Italian) and 0.28 F0.5 (Chinese). Differences of ~1 point or less, and all MT
  differences here, should be treated as noise. The paired bootstrap tests in SIGNIFICANCE.md (added
  2026-10-09) only cover which test sentences were sampled, not this run-to-run variation, so a small
  difference can be significant there and still be training noise.
* The Italian test set is small (81 essays).
* The it+zh run (can one model learn both?) was not run.
* Not measured: whether GEC training makes the model silently 'fix' errors in the source when translating.
* Chinese scores: ChERRANT scores the references against themselves at F0.5 99.94, not 100 (Italian: exactly
  100). The gap most likely comes from a newer OpenCC version; it shifts absolute Chinese scores by ~0.1 at
  most and equally for all runs.
