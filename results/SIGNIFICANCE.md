# Significance tests

Paired bootstrap, 1000 resamples per test (seed 12345), produced by `eval/significance.py`. Each cell is the difference in score (second model minus first) with its two-sided p-value. **Bold** = significant at p < 0.05: the 95% interval of the difference excludes zero. With 1000 resamples the smallest p-value that can be shown is 0.002 (written ≤). Intervals are in `significance.json`.

- **control - official**: the effect of the extra training on its own
- **it - control**, **zh - control**: the effect of adding Italian or Chinese GEC data
- **it - official**, **zh - official**: both together

There are 320 tests here, so at p < 0.05 a few would come out significant by chance alone. Treat isolated p-values just under 0.05 with care, especially among the per-pair rows.

The bootstrap only covers the choice of test sentences. It does not cover the run-to-run variation of training itself (one run per condition; two identical training runs differed by 0.40 Italian F0.5, see RESULTS.txt). A small difference can be significant here and still be training noise, so read significant differences much smaller than that, such as most translation and APE ones, with care.
